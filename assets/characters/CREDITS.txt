man.glb, woman.glb

Bodies, skins, eyes and eyebrows: MakeHuman (https://static.makehumancommunity.org),
the makehuman_system_assets pack, built with MPFB for Blender. Both are
public domain (CC0 1.0).

Underwear, MakeHuman community assets:
  man: "Boxer Shorts" by WojackOWL, CC-BY 4.0,
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/boxer_shorts.html
       (its material trimmed to the base colour texture)
  woman: panties and top by wolgade (underwear01 pack), public domain (CC0 1.0)

Animations: Universal Animation Library (Standard) by Quaternius,
https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library, public domain (CC0 1.0).

Idle, walk, run, jumps, punch and pick up: Mixamo animations
(https://www.mixamo.com), retargeted onto the bodies. Free to use in games
under Adobe's terms, but not to redistribute as standalone files; the raw
downloads stay out of the repo (build/mixamo is ignored).

Wardrobe (man/, woman/, skins/, wardrobe.json): MakeHuman assets, all public
domain (CC0 1.0): the system assets' hair, shoes, suits and skins, and the
shirts01, pants01 and glasses01 packs
(https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html), fitted to
the bodies with MPFB.

Faction clothes and masks, MakeHuman community assets (CC-BY 4.0), fitted
to the bodies with MPFB and some repainted in the factions' colours:
  "Elvs Male Coveralls 1" by Elvaerwyn (Crew coveralls),
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/elvs_male_coveralls_1.html
  "Elvs Male Trench Coat1" by Elvaerwyn (Registrar coat),
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/elvs_male_trench_coat1.html
  "Big Desert Poncho" by brkurt (Desert poncho),
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/big_desert_poncho.html
  "uniform jacket (train driver)" and "uniform pants (train driver)" by
       punkduck (Charter jacket and trousers),
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/uniform_jacket_train_driver.html
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/uniform_pants_train_driver.html
  "Goggles" by punkduck, http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/goggles.html
  "Gas Mask" by Mathias_Gredal (Rebreather),
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/gas_mask.html
  "Surgical Mask" by WojackOWL (Dust mask),
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/surgical_mask.html
  Each is listed by number in tools/makehuman/cast.py's COMMUNITY; their
  materials are trimmed to the base colour texture.
