man.glb, woman.glb

Bodies, skins, eyes and eyebrows: MakeHuman (https://static.makehumancommunity.org),
the makehuman_system_assets pack, built with MPFB for Blender. Both are
public domain (CC0 1.0).

Underwear, MakeHuman community assets:
  man: "Boxer Shorts" by WojackOWL, CC-BY 4.0,
       http://www.makehumancommunity.org/clothes/boxer_shorts.html
       (its material trimmed to the base colour texture)
  woman: panties and top by wolgade (underwear01 pack), public domain (CC0 1.0)

Animations: Universal Animation Library (Standard) by Quaternius,
https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library, public domain (CC0 1.0).

Idle, walk, run, jumps, punch and pick up: Mixamo animations
(https://www.mixamo.com), retargeted onto the bodies. Free to use in games
under Adobe's terms, but not to redistribute as standalone files; the raw
downloads stay out of the repo (build/mixamo is ignored).
