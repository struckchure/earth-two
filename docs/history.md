# History

Dates count in years After Landfall (AL). The game opens in 88 AL, two years
before the colony charter expires. The rows run oldest first, because the story
reads forward.

| Year | Event | What it left behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 AL | **Landfall.** The colony ship *Second Light* grounds on a plateau with 4,200 settlers and Corvane staff. | The Hull: the ship itself, which became the first city. |
| 0–38 AL | **The Company Years.** Domes go up and terraforming plants start thickening the air. A supply ship arrives about every six years. | Corvane scrip, company housing, half-finished terraformers. |
| 38 AL | **The Silence.** Corvane goes bankrupt on Earth. No more ships and no more orders, and the company staff are stranded. | Company managers who become the Charter Families. |
| 38–41 AL | **The Shortage.** Air filters and seed stock run low and there are riots. The ship's engineering crew lock down the air plant and keep it running. | The Crew's claim on air and power, and a lot of grudges. |
| 41 AL | **The Accord of the Hull.** Every faction agrees that any deal filed in public will be honoured, and bonded contractors may enforce it. | The Exchange and its licence tiers. |
| 41–60 AL | **The Long Quiet.** The colony is self-sufficient but poor. Terraforming stalls at "breathable with a mask". | The Fringe: farms and salvage camps outside the domes. |
| 60 AL | **The Drifters.** Independent long-haul freighters start arriving every few years with goods and migrants who pay for passage in debt. | The Late Arrivals, a new underclass. Every player is one of them. |
| 88 AL | **Now.** A drifter brings word that Corvane's creditors have bought the charter. A receivership ship is on its way to enforce it before 90 AL. | The main plot. |

## The charter clause

The clause everyone is fighting over: if the company doesn't exercise the
charter by 90 AL, title to the planet passes to "the resident colony". Nobody
agrees on who counts as the resident colony. See [Story](story.md).

## The air

The outside air is breathable with a mask. Nobody needs a full suit, so the
whole Fringe is free roam on foot.
