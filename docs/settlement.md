# The settlement

The Red's people live in a handful of seats scattered across open desert, a
real journey apart: 8 to 15 km, an hour or more on foot and a quarter of that
by hauler. Between them is the Fringe: red dust, dunes, rock and wind. Like
Dune's sietches and its city, each seat is somebody's ground, and getting from
one to another is the first thing anyone has to work out.

The first city is **Landfall**, built around the grounded colony ship *Second
Light*. It's small, dense and vertical, which suits the traversal moves the
game already has (vault, mantle, wall-kick, ladders, slide). Build the Hull
first, then add the other seats once the contract loop feels good.

Everything legal passes through Landfall's South gate, and everything
off-book goes around it. The roads are where escort, smuggling and hijacking
contracts are won or lost.

## Seats

| Seat | Where | Look | What happens there | Run by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Landfall: the Hull** | Under the dome, in the ship | The ship's decks cut open into streets: catwalks, ladders, shafts, market stalls in old corridors | The Exchange, markets, repair runs, a new player's first room in the Stacks; the most climbing and vaulting | Crew (lower decks), Registrars (the Exchange) |
| **Landfall: Charter Row** | Under the dome, north of the Hull | Old company buildings kept clean: white walls, gardens under glass, private guards | Legal deals, audits, theft and break-in jobs | Charter Families |
| **The Pads** | 10 km east of Landfall, at the end of the caravan road | The old landing field, turned freight port: pads, containers, cranes, fuel tanks, loaders; open air, masks on | Where drifters land and every new player arrives; hauling, buying off-world goods on drifter days, Quiet Book fronts | Nobody; contested |
| **The Fringers' hold** | 12 km south of Landfall, down the Fringe track | A hold dug in among the dunes: patched greenhouses, crop beds, water tanks, wind farm, salvage claims and the wrecked terraformer | Food, seed, salvage; escort and salvage contracts; the Fringers' politics | Fringers |
| **The Quiet Book's haven** | 9 km north-west of Landfall, in the canyons; no road | Containers, shacks and a dead terminal tucked out of sight | Fences, forgers, off-book credit; somewhere to lie low | The Quiet Book |

The outside air is breathable with a mask, so the whole Fringe is free roam on
foot, if you can stand the walk. Players start on foot; haulers and bikes are
earned, and caravans run between the seats for a fare. For who runs each seat,
see [Factions](factions.md).
