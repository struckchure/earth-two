# The settlement

The first city is **Landfall**, built around the grounded colony ship *Second
Light*. It's small, dense and vertical, which suits the traversal moves the game
already has (vault, mantle, wall-kick, ladders, slide). Build the Hull first,
then add the other districts once the contract loop feels good.

```
                         THE FRINGE
            outside the domes: masks on, no law

        . - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - .   dome line
      '            +-------------------+            '
     '             |    Charter Row    |             '
    '              +---------+---------+              '
   '                         |                         '
  '   +----------------------+-------------+  +--------+ '
  '   |  THE HULL                          |--|  The   | '
  '   |  upper decks:  the Stacks          |  |  Pads  | '
  '   |  middle decks: markets, Exchange   |  +--------+ '
   '  |  lower decks:  the Crew, air plant |            '
    ' +-----------------+------------------+           '
     '                  |  gate checks                '
       ' - - - - - - - -#- - - - - - - - - - - - - - '
+-------+   caravan road  South gate   salvage haul  +----------------+
| Farms |--------------------#-----------------------| Salvage fields |
+-------+                                            +----------------+
```

Everything legal passes through the Hull, and everything off-book goes around
it. The South gate is the only way in from the Fringe, so it's where smuggling
contracts are won or lost.

## Districts

| District | Look | What happens there | Run by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Hull** | The ship's decks cut open into streets: catwalks, ladders, shafts, market stalls in old corridors | The Exchange, markets, repair runs, a new player's first room in the Stacks; the most climbing and vaulting | Crew (below), Registrars (middle) |
| **Charter Row** | Old company buildings kept clean: white walls, gardens under glass, private guards | Legal deals, audits, theft and break-in jobs | Charter Families |
| **The Pads** | Landing pads turned into freight yards: containers, cranes, fuel tanks, gangs of loaders | Where drifters land and new players arrive; hauling, buying off-world goods on drifter days, Quiet Book fronts | Nobody; contested |
| **The Fringe** | Red dust, wind farms, patched greenhouses, wrecked terraformers on the horizon | Escort, salvage claims, smuggling; long sightlines and storms | Fringers |

The outside air is breathable with a mask, so the whole Fringe is free roam on
foot. For who runs each district, see [Factions](factions.md).
