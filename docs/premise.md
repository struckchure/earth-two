# Premise

Earth Two is a colony its company abandoned. Nobody governs it, and every
deal is a contract.

Eighty-eight years ago the Corvane Colonial Company landed 4,200 settlers here
under a 90-year charter. Fifty years ago Corvane went bankrupt on Earth and the
supply ships stopped. The colonists kept going on their own. Nobody here says
"Earth Two" any more except on paper. The colonists call the planet **the
Red**, after the red soil and the dust that gets into everything.

What replaced the company is **the Exchange**, a public registry where anyone
can file a contract and anyone with a licence can enforce one. Legal means
"filed". Illegal means "off the books". See [The Exchange](exchange.md).

Players arrive in debt on cargo haulers, and every one of them owes their
passage to the same creditor, **Ada Vellér**. They work their way up through
contracts, trade and reputation.

**The twist:** two years before the charter runs out, a ship from Earth is
coming to claim the planet back. Whoever brokers that deal decides who owns
Earth Two. See [Story](story.md).

**A shared world.** Earth Two is multiplayer. Each player arrives at their own
time and plays the same story, while the market, contracts, property and debts
are shared by everyone. See [Shared world](shared-world.md).

## Tone

A grounded, used future. People are practical, tired and quietly funny. Power
belongs to whoever controls air, water and paperwork, not to whoever has the
most guns. The world isn't evil, it's transactional: everybody has a price, and
most of them are fair.
