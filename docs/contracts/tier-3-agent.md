# Tier 3 — Agent

Agent requires Standing and a faction sponsor. The player can post work, hire
NPC crews and hold a warehouse. Their personal system contract remains their
responsibility; a hired crew helps fulfil it rather than becoming another
player entitled to take it. These entries are drafts.

## T3-01 — Four names on a shift

**Issuer:** Chief Oduya. **Kind:** filed crew commission; repeatable.

> Four stations, four workers. Put names beside the jobs before you start,
> and put wages beside the names before you finish. The plant cannot run
> on whoever happened to be nearest.

**Work:** hire an NPC crew to service four assigned stations, provide the
allocated parts and submit each worker's completion reading and wage receipt.
The player chooses the crew and schedule and is responsible for unresolved work.

**Draft terms:** 900 marks; 90-mark bond; six real hours. A separate 400-mark
crew-wage advance funds the listed work. Pay requires completed checks and
paid wages. Default loses bond and Standing; unaccounted advance is a debt
capped at 400 marks. Wage claims and recovered funds must be reconciled so the
same missing money is not charged twice.

**Personal assignment:** four reserved maintenance tickets and a separate
crew budget. Repeats use fresh tickets; an already serviced station is excluded.

**Needs:** hiring, schedules, crew outcomes, budgets and wage receipts.

## T3-02 — Nothing left on the dock

**Issuer:** Ada Vellér. **Kind:** filed warehouse and haul commission; repeatable.

> I have paid for storage from tomorrow. Arrange the movement today and
> account for the goods yourself. A warehouse key is useful, but it is
> not an inventory.

**Work:** arrange transport and storage for twelve personally allocated
electronics cases from the Pads to the specified Hull warehouse bays. The
player can hire crews, but must reconcile the dispatch and receiving records.

**Draft terms:** 1,200 marks; 120-mark bond; eight real hours. The fee covers
the player's service; transport and storage allowances are listed separately
and must be accounted for. Default loses bond and Standing. The offer caps
cargo liability at 800 marks after returned or recovered goods are counted.

**Personal assignment:** distinct cargo IDs and reserved bay capacity for the
named instance. Another agent's delivery cannot occupy its promised capacity
without the issuer resolving the conflict.

**Needs:** warehouse holdings, crew dispatch, transport quotes and inventory.

## T3-03 — Before the price changes

**Issuer:** Maro Teska. **Kind:** filed procurement and dispatch; repeatable.

> The buyers want food; the growers want filters. Arrange the exchange
> while both still mean it. If you change the quantities, tell the people
> who have already loaded their carts.

**Work:** procure twenty tested filter cartridges within the listed ceiling,
arrange the corresponding produce shipment and obtain both parties' receiving
records. The contract publishes its permitted substitutions and spend limits.

**Draft terms:** 1,000 marks; 100-mark bond; six real hours. A restricted
1,000-mark purchasing float is separate from the fee. Default loses bond and
Standing; missing float is repayable up to the amount advanced. A market
change does not authorise spending beyond the accepted limit.

**Personal assignment:** reserve a produce lot, buyers' orders and purchasing
budget. Variants follow actual demand and never resell a lot already promised.

**Needs:** trading, linked receipts, crew transport and permitted substitutions.

## T3-04 — Two signatures, one tank

**Issuer:** Registrar Ines Kade. **Kind:** filed brokered deal; once per career.

> The Crew can deliver the water. The Family can release the storage.
> Neither has signed the same quantity as the other. Bring me one agreement
> they will both stand behind.

**Work:** negotiate a specified Crew water allocation into a Charter Family
storage lease, obtain both signatures and file matching quantity, duration and
payment schedules. A document signed on inconsistent terms does not count.

**Draft terms:** 600 marks; 60-mark bond; one real day. Default loses the bond
and Standing. The player may negotiate within the published limits but cannot
pledge either party's property or funds beyond their mandate.

**Personal assignment:** a distinct allocation, storage bay and agreement
docket. This is the player's qualifying deal, not a shared promotion mission
another person can complete for them.

**Career consequence:** the verified deal satisfies the Exchange's brokered-deal
requirement for Broker. It does not waive any other eligibility or standing
requirements. Ordinary Agent work remains available before and after it.

**Needs:** negotiation, party mandates, signatures and licence progression.
