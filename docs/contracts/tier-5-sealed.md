# Tier 5 — Sealed

Sealed authority is an Act 3 story gate: the player can file on behalf of a
faction and has a voice in the charter negotiation. Routine faction work
continues alongside the once-only final personal filing. These are drafts;
no completion here should repeatedly flip the shared world's ending.

## T5-01 — Air in writing

**Issuer:** Chief Oduya. **Kind:** filed Crew mandate; repeatable.

> A promise of air is easy to applaud. This is the maintenance schedule,
> the reserve allocation and the names on the night shift. Get those into
> the agreement. We can breathe the argument afterwards.

**Work:** use the Crew's specific mandate to file a maintenance and reserve
agreement for the assigned dome ward. Verify staffing, costs and allocation
limits, obtain the counterparties' signatures and lodge the accepted schedule.

**Draft terms:** 3,000 marks; 150-mark bond; two real days. Default loses bond
and Standing. The mandate does not grant ownership of the air plant or power
to cut off other wards. Unsupported commitments must be disputed, not treated
as resources the Crew has already promised.

**Personal assignment:** a distinct ward, reserve allocation and filing
reference. Repeats cover fresh schedules or other wards with actual demand.

**Needs:** faction authority, schedules, shared resource allocations and signatures.

## T5-02 — An inventory of residents

**Issuer:** Registrar Ines Kade. **Kind:** filed representation record; repeatable.

> The charter asks who resides here. A deed answers who owns something.
> For this ward, record both. Leave space for the people who have neither
> a deed nor someone willing to speak for them.

**Work:** gather the allocated ward's residency evidence and representation
mandates, distinguish ownership from residence and file an auditable register
for the negotiation. Disputed entries retain their objections.

**Draft terms:** 2,800 marks; 140-mark bond; two real days. Default loses bond
and Standing. Fabricated mandates or suppressed objections create a dispute;
the fee does not buy the right to manufacture votes or erase residents.

**Personal assignment:** one reserved ward register or batch of new entries.
A second Sealed player receives another batch, not another payment for the
same verification already filed.

**Needs:** residency records, consent, representation and audit trails.

## T5-03 — A name of our own

**Issuer:** the Late Arrivals' committee. **Kind:** filed faction mandate;
repeatable after organisation in Act 2.

> We know who paid passage. Now we need the names of the people who agreed
> to stand together. Read the mandate back before you file it. Being
> counted for somebody else's argument is not much of an improvement.

**Work:** verify a personally allocated group of member mandates, explain
their recorded scope to the signers and file the committee's representation
for the stated claim. Membership is voluntary and distinct from an arrival's
passage debt.

**Draft terms:** 2,000 marks; 100-mark bond; two real days. Default loses bond
and Standing. False consent or use beyond the mandate can cost the committee's
trust and open an Exchange dispute. Payment comes from a reserved committee
work fund, not newly added passage debt on the members.

**Personal assignment:** a separate mandate batch and representation docket.
Once verified, those signatures cannot be sold again as newly gathered ones.

**Story condition:** the Late Arrivals were organised in Act 2. This work
supports the hidden-ending conditions but does not substitute for organising
them earlier.

**Needs:** faction organisation, consent, mandates and the Late Claim flags.

## T5-04 — The resident colony

**Issuer:** the player's chosen faction; Kade witnesses filed settlement routes.
**Kind:** final personal charter; once per player per season.

> This draft decides whose promises the Exchange will recognise after the
> ship leaves. Read the exclusions as carefully as the rights. When you
> decide, both will have your name beside them.

**Work:** assemble the evidence and valid mandates required by the player's
chosen settlement and complete its final personal decision. Filed routes
produce a charter filing; Open Sky requires its own rejection/destruction
terms and completion record. The offer presents the faction's proposal at
the terminal; the player reviews
the actual settlement before the final commitment.

**Draft terms:** settlement-specific consideration and property outcomes are
declared in the chosen proposal; no generic repeatable cash fee or bond in
this writing pass. The deadline is the shared season end. An unfinished proposal
does not count in the season tally. Filing consequences remain reviewable as
part of the proposal, not an undisclosed penalty.

**Personal assignment:** one charter docket and final decision per player.
It determines that player's epilogue, faction standing and property outcomes.
It does not overwrite another player's ending or repeatedly change the shared
settlement. The season's final filings are tallied for the next world.

**Possible routes:** The Title, The Commons, The Ledger Republic, Open Sky,
The Sale and, with the Act 2 conditions met, The Late Claim. Each route needs
its own detailed final offer before implementation; this entry defines the
common shape, not those six complete settlements.

**Needs:** charter proposals, mandate validation, endings and season tallying.
