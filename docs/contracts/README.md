# Contract review book

System contracts are personal assignments drawn from a pool of templates.
Every issued contract belongs to one player and can be taken only by that
player. Two people can receive the same kind of work, but never the same
contract instance, cargo allocation or payment. See [System contract rules](system-contracts.md).

This folder is the first writing pass for review. **First filing is playable
in the local prototype. The other 26 entries are drafts**, not jobs already
running in the game. Multiplayer ownership checks and the full tier pools are
also design work still to implement. Prices, bonds, deadlines and dialogue
below are proposals, except First filing's existing terms.

The pool stays stocked at every tier. A higher licence can still take eligible
lower-tier work. A player has **one ongoing contract at a time**; fresh offers
do not give them a second active slot. Story-gated offers supplement ordinary
work rather than replacing the pool.

## Review order

Start with [tier 0](tier-0-unlisted.md) for the opening experience, then
[tier 1](tier-1-hand.md) for the everyday tone. Read the higher tiers to check
how a person doing errands becomes someone responsible for other people's
work and, eventually, someone's rights.

[Voice and form](tone.md) explains the writing approach. [System contract
rules](system-contracts.md) defines ownership, replenishment and shared-world
targets. The existing [contract types](../contracts.md), [Exchange](../exchange.md)
and [story](../story.md) remain the wider design references.

## Decisions to review in this pass

- **Opening journey:** First filing still crosses the 10 km caravan road.
  Local cash-paying labour gives arrivals a way to earn a caravan fare; the
  first job itself keeps its existing debt-credit terms and grants no vehicle.
- **Economics:** draft pay and liability caps need balancing against travel,
  consumables, crew wages and the shared economy. A draft number is not a
  settled market price.
- **Licence gates:** this pass follows the Exchange's table. Clearing the
  passage debt earns Hand; ten clean contracts earn Contractor; Agent also
  needs a sponsor. Act 1 continues through Hand to Contractor.
- **Tone:** review whether each issuer sounds like a person with a practical
  problem, and whether the pressure is visible in the terms rather than an
  exposition speech.
