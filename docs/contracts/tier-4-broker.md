# Tier 4 — Broker

The player can broker multi-party deals and enforce filed obligations. A
system collection is assigned personally to its collector; the underlying
debt claim is reserved so it is not paid or recovered twice. All entries are
drafts. Receiver Hale's offer requires the Receivership's arrival in orbit.

## T4-01 — The amount still owing

**Issuer:** Ada Vellér. **Kind:** filed collection; repeatable.

> The amount is on the first page. The payments are on the second. Collect
> the difference, please. I have no use for an enforcement record that
> forgets what a person has already paid.

**Work:** recover the assigned unpaid balance, or the listed pledged goods at
their accepted valuation, from a named debtor. File the receipt or settlement
with the Exchange. The order identifies lawful scope and any person-custody
authority separately; it does not invent unlimited authority over the debtor.

**Draft terms:** 1,800 marks; 144-mark bond; one real day. Recovery money belongs
to the creditor, not the broker. Default loses bond and Standing. The broker
must account for sums actually collected; further liability is capped at those
unreturned sums, not the whole debt simply because recovery failed.

**Personal assignment:** one reserved debt claim and enforcement docket.
Other collectors receive different cases. A recorded repayment reduces the
claim immediately, even if it comes from someone outside this assignment.

**Needs:** debt ledgers, debtor behaviour, settlements, combat and arbitration.
Violence can be lethal and leaves consequences in the shared world.

## T4-02 — Both copies of the truth

**Issuer:** Registrar Ines Kade. **Kind:** filed arbitration evidence; repeatable.

> Both receipts bear a valid seal. They cannot both describe the delivery
> the parties are arguing about. Find the dispatch record and preserve
> each copy. I need to know where they stopped agreeing.

**Work:** reconcile the two parties' documents against an assigned shipment,
interview its receiver and submit the original evidence with a report that
separates facts from disputed claims. The player supplies evidence; Kade rules.

**Draft terms:** 1,500 marks; 120-mark bond; one real day. Default loses bond
and Standing. Fabricated or destroyed evidence can create an arbitration case
against the broker, with document liability capped at 500 marks in this offer.

**Personal assignment:** a unique dispute docket and allocated originals.
Other players can observe its public filing, but cannot submit those originals
as their own system assignment.

**Needs:** interviews, document custody, evidence comparison and rulings.

## T4-03 — The missing line

**Issuer:** Registrar Ines Kade. **Kind:** filed claims audit; repeatable case
work within the Late Arrival story thread.

> The arrival paid to lodge the claim. The fee is here; the claim is not.
> Trace who handled this docket before somebody tells us the applicant
> never existed.

**Work:** trace one allocated missing-claim case from its arrival receipt
through the Exchange's custody records, recover its surviving papers and file
the discrepancy. Preserve the claimant's consent and evidence chain.

**Draft terms:** 2,000 marks; 160-mark bond; two real days. Default loses bond
and Standing. Exposure may anger whoever benefited from the missing filing.
The terms do not authorise taking unrelated claims or changing ownership.

**Personal assignment:** a distinct claimant, claim parcel and custody trail.
The larger conspiracy is shared story context; this player's evidence and
personal story progress belong to their instance.

**Story condition:** the missing-filings thread has been opened. One audit
does not resolve every player's investigation or reveal every Corvane agent.

**Needs:** claims, custody trails, investigation and personal story flags.

## T4-04 — An orderly inventory

**Issuer:** Receiver Hale. **Kind:** filed title audit; repeatable after arrival.

> We can argue over what the charter permits once we agree what is here.
> Inventory the assigned parcels and attach the residents' objections to
> the same record. Uncertainty is expensive for them as well as for us.

**Work:** audit the designated Corvane title docket against occupied property,
local filings and residents' statements. Return an indexed report including
objections, rather than silently replacing them with the company's version.

**Draft terms:** 2,500 marks; 200-mark bond; two real days. Default loses bond
and Standing. Access is limited to the listed parcels and records. Claimants
keep ownership until the dispute process actually decides otherwise.

**Personal assignment:** separate parcel groups and dockets, with reserved
evidence access. The system does not promise the same paid audit to a queue
of brokers after its factual work is already complete.

**Story condition:** Hale is present following the ship's arrival in orbit.
Accepting an audit does not itself choose the Corvane ending.

**Needs:** property records, access mandates, interviews and competing claims.
