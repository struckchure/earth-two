# Tier 2 — Contractor

Ten clean filed contracts qualify a Hand as a Contractor. The work now extends
across the Fringe and can put other people's goods or movement in the player's
care. All four entries are repeatable drafts, with proposed terms.

## T2-01 — Keep the last cart in sight

**Issuer:** Maro Teska. **Kind:** filed escort.

> The first cart always arrives. It is the last one that brings back the
> stories. Stay with the grain wagon until the South gate takes its papers.
> If it stops, find out why before the others disappear.

**Work:** escort the assigned grain caravan from the Fringers' hold to
Landfall's South gate. Keep the marked rear cart accounted for and obtain
the gate receipt for its cargo. The caravan provides its own transport;
the order does not grant the player a vehicle.

**Draft terms:** 1,000 marks; 100-mark bond; four real hours. Default loses
bond and Standing. Any cargo claim needs evidence of the loss and arbitration;
the order caps further liability at 500 marks. A clean escort raises Standing.

**Personal assignment:** one caravan departure and rear-cart reference is
assigned to one contractor. Other players may pass or interfere in the shared
world, but cannot accept this escort or collect its pay.

**Needs:** caravan pathing, stops, danger responses and gate receipts.

## T2-02 — What the sand left

**Issuer:** Maro Teska. **Kind:** filed salvage claim.

> The claim covers the east compartment. The rest belongs to someone else,
> however abandoned it looks. Bring the useful metal and the part numbers.
> I can sell one without pretending to own the other.

**Work:** extract the three specified components from the reserved compartment
of a wrecked terraformer, record their serials and deliver them to the hold's
salvage counter. The claim marks the boundary of permitted work.

**Draft terms:** 900 marks; 90-mark bond; four real hours. Default loses bond
and Standing. Damaging or taking goods outside the claim is separately
disputable. Liability for the three allocated components is capped at 300 marks.

**Personal assignment:** reserve that compartment or component lot for this
instance. Other system assignments use different lots. Never promise the same
intact component after someone has already stripped it.

**Needs:** salvage ownership, extraction, condition and delivery records.
Traversal and pickup provide the physical work.

## T2-03 — A better copy

**Issuer:** Ledger. **Kind:** off-book forgery drop.

> There are two copies. One has the right seal and the wrong date. One has
> the right date and no seal. Put this third copy where the clerk expects
> an answer, then leave the originals where they can still be found.

**Work:** place the supplied forged document into the allocated Exchange
record sleeve during its access window. Preserve the original records and
return the sleeve's receiving reference to the Quiet Book contact.

**Draft terms:** eight filters; no Exchange bond; two real hours. Failure
forfeits pay and damages Quiet. Detection can damage Standing and open a
Registrar investigation. No additional replacement debt is hidden in the offer.

**Personal assignment:** one sleeve, forged copy and access window per player.
A sleeve modified by another case cannot simultaneously be promised as unchanged.

**Needs:** record access, timed windows, forgery checks and exposure tracking.

## T2-04 — One dark warehouse

**Issuer:** Ledger. **Kind:** off-book sabotage.

> Their lights can go out. The air cannot. Isolate the warehouse feed,
> photograph the meter and leave the life-support circuit alone. I am
> buying a delay, not a room full of bodies.

**Work:** disable the assigned warehouse's nonessential power feed for the
specified loading window, then deliver the meter record. The order identifies
protected air, water and emergency circuits that must remain live.

**Draft terms:** 1,200 marks; no Exchange bond; ninety real minutes to perform
the work. Pay requires the agreed outage and intact protected circuits.
Failure damages Quiet; exposure damages Standing. Harm outside the agreed
scope creates consequences beyond losing the fee.

**Personal assignment:** reserve one circuit and loading window. Repeats
require a fresh authorised window or another target; an existing outage is
not another completed sabotage.

**Needs:** power state, circuit separation, recording and consequences.
