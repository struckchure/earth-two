# Tier 0 — Unlisted

The player has no licence, no vehicle and a 2,000-mark passage debt. Ordinary
work needs no bond. Local cash-paying jobs let an arrival earn food, filters
or a caravan fare before attempting the longer journey to Landfall.

First filing is playable. All other entries below are drafts; their prices,
terms and terminal copy are for review.

## T0-01 — First filing

**Issuer:** Ada Vellér. **Kind:** sponsored day labour, once per arrival career.

> Welcome. Your passage is on my books: 2,000 marks. Deliver this filing, and
> I will credit the first 150.

**Work:** accept at the arrivals terminal on the Pads, collect the sealed
arrival filing and hand it to a Registrar counter in the Exchange at Landfall.
The route is the 10 km caravan road. Delivery requires a handover, not merely
reaching the building.

**Existing terms:** 150 marks credited directly to Ada's passage debt; no
bond, deadline or default penalty. No cash reward, Standing, Quiet, licence
or vehicle is granted. Debt becomes 1,850 marks on successful delivery.

**Personal assignment:** each arrival has their own filing and receipt. One
person delivering theirs must never deliver or consume another's.

**Repeat:** never within the same arrival career. The template is shared; the
issued obligation belongs to one player. Multiplayer instance ownership is
still to implement beyond the local prototype.

## T0-02 — Before the ramp closes

**Issuer:** Chief Oduya. **Kind:** day labour; repeatable local haul.

> Blue marks on the cases. Put those six beside the Crew's truck, not the
> six someone else has signed for. We can count them there.

**Work:** move six allocated filter cases from the drifter-day receiving
table to the Crew's marked pickup rack, both on the Pads. Confirm the count
at the receiving terminal. No vehicle is needed.

**Draft terms:** 120 marks in cash; no bond or time limit. Incomplete work
pays nothing. Return unused cases when leaving the order; missing cases may
create a replacement debt capped at 120 marks, disclosed before acceptance.

**Personal assignment:** the receiving table issues a distinct tagged lot to
each taker. Progress counts those case IDs, not any six props on the ground.
Repeat with a fresh incoming lot; never repay a lot already sorted.

**Plays with:** pickup, short routes and handover. **Needs:** owned cargo,
counts and cash settlement.

## T0-03 — Clean hands

**Issuer:** Maro Teska. **Kind:** day labour; repeatable equipment service.

> Seed is cheap here. Seed that survived the trip from Earth isn't. Clean
> the housing and seat the seal. I'll pay when the case holds pressure.

**Work:** service the player's allocated imported seed case at the Pads'
workbench, then run its pressure check and return it to the receiving rack.
The order provides the gasket and a borrowed tool; no purchases are required.

**Draft terms:** 80 marks in cash; no bond or deadline. No pay until the case
passes its check. Return the case and tool on withdrawal; no penalty for an
honest failed check with the employer's equipment returned.

**Personal assignment:** the fault and case serial belong to this order.
Another worker's repair does not complete it. Repeats use another case.

**Plays with:** interact and the working pose. **Needs:** a repair objective,
verification and a returnable tool record. Kneeling alone is not completion.

## T0-04 — The third bottle

**Issuer:** Ledger. **Kind:** off-book smuggling; repeatable.

> Three bottles came off that ship. Two made it onto the manifest. The third
> is medicine too. Deliver it to the Hull dispensary and let them decide
> what it is worth.

**Work:** collect one personally allocated unfiled medicine case from a Quiet
Book front on the Pads and deliver it to the specified dispensary contact in
the Hull. The South gate checks can expose it; an alternate route or disguise
is part of the work.

**Draft terms:** four filter cartridges; no Exchange bond; two real hours
from acceptance. A lost case forfeits pay, damages Quiet and leaves a capped
four-filter replacement obligation to Ledger. A clean delivery raises Quiet,
not Standing. It does not sell the player's passage debt to Ledger.

**Personal assignment:** a fresh medicine lot and receiving reference for
each player. Repeat only while the issuer can supply and fund another lot.

**Plays with:** traversal and wardrobe. **Needs:** cargo, gate checks,
receiving contacts and off-book settlement.

## T0-05 — Names in the right order

**Issuer:** Registrar Ines Kade. **Kind:** neutral day labour; repeatable.

> These envelopes have arrived. Their receiving slips have taken a different
> route. Match the references and return the batch in order. Nobody needs
> a new identity because two trays were put beside the wrong desk.

**Work:** reconcile three assigned document envelopes with their receiving
slips at the Pads' arrivals desk. Return the correct links and the complete
batch to the terminal. The work verifies custody; it does not change anyone's
name, identity or claim.

**Draft terms:** 60 marks in cash; no bond, deadline or default penalty.
Unfinished or incorrect work pays nothing. The issuer retains the documents;
the player does not need to buy equipment or travel to another seat.

**Personal assignment:** one unprocessed batch per player, with distinct
references. Repeats require new receiving records or genuine corrections,
not another fee for a batch already reconciled.

**Pool role:** ordinary, zero-bond work available across licence tiers without
a faction sponsor. The Registry keeps a funded work reserve for this fallback.
It grants no Standing or licence and is not a filed courier contract.

**Needs:** document matching, custody verification and cash settlement.
