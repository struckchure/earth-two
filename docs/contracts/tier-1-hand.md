# Tier 1 — Hand

Hand is earned by clearing the passage debt. Filed work now has a bond and a
public completion record. These are draft offers, not implemented jobs. Their
deadlines begin on acceptance and use the world's real clock.

## T1-01 — A seal is not a signature

**Issuer:** Registrar Ines Kade. **Kind:** filed courier; repeatable.

> The seal says this came from the Exchange. It does not say anyone received
> it. Take the notice to Charter Row and bring back the recipient's mark.
> That is what I can file.

**Work:** deliver the assigned sealed notice from the Exchange to its named
Charter Row office, then return the acknowledgment. An arrival at the office
without the acknowledgment does not satisfy the order.

**Draft terms:** 200 marks; 30-mark bond; one real hour. A verified return
releases pay and bond and raises Standing. Default loses the bond and
Standing; an unreturned notice must be surrendered or disputed.

**Personal assignment:** the notice, recipient and acknowledgment reference
are unique to the player. Repeats change the notice and recipient docket.

**Plays with:** short traversal routes and interaction. **Needs:** receipt
items, a work clock, bonds and filed reputation.

## T1-02 — Filters by the dozen

**Issuer:** Chief Oduya. **Kind:** filed haul; repeatable.

> Twelve cases are leaving the Pads. Twelve should reach the Hull. If the
> numbers disagree, bring me the disagreement before you bring me a bill.

**Work:** deliver twelve tagged cases from a personally reserved Pads lot to
the assigned Hull warehouse rack. The terminal lists the case IDs and allows
several trips. The player must arrange lawful transport they can afford.

**Draft terms:** 400 marks; 60-mark bond; three real hours. Pay and bond are
released after all twelve are counted in. Default loses the bond and
Standing. Missing cargo liability is capped at 600 marks; recovered cargo
reduces that liability before a collection case is considered.

**Personal assignment:** reserve the cargo lot and receiving capacity at
issue. Another player's haul never advances this count.

**Plays with:** pickup and routing. **Needs:** cargo accounting, warehouse
receipts, transport arrangements and loss settlement.

## T1-03 — The valve that sings

**Issuer:** Chief Oduya. **Kind:** filed repair run; repeatable.

> The noise is in the bypass valve, not the fan. Bring the marked part down
> the lower-deck ladder, fit it and take a reading. I have already paid
> someone to listen to it.

**Work:** collect the replacement allocated to this fault report, reach the
marked lower-deck valve station, install it and submit a pressure reading
within the specified safe range. Supply the old valve with the report.

**Draft terms:** 500 marks; 75-mark bond; ninety real minutes. Default loses
bond and Standing. An unreturned replacement part creates liability capped
at 200 marks. A verified repair raises Standing.

**Personal assignment:** one live fault ticket reserves one valve until the
order closes. A repaired valve cannot generate another paid repair; the next
instance uses a different fault.

**Plays with:** ladders, climbing and interaction. **Needs:** faults, part
installation and actual readings, rather than a pose timer alone.

## T1-04 — Cells, not promises

**Issuer:** Chief Oduya. **Kind:** filed buy order; repeatable.

> Five working cells, no more than forty marks each. A cheap empty cell is
> still an empty cell. Bring the test slips with them.

**Work:** source five specified power cells under the unit-price ceiling,
verify charge and deliver the cells and purchase records to the Crew. The
issuer advances a ring-fenced 200-mark purchasing float.

**Draft terms:** 240 marks for the work; 36-mark bond; two real hours.
Unused purchasing funds return to the issuer. Default loses bond and Standing;
unaccounted float is a separate liability capped at the 200 advanced. The
float is not the player's profit.

**Personal assignment:** a separate procurement lot, float and consumption
ticket for each taker. Repeats require a fresh funded demand.

**Plays with:** travel and comparison shopping. **Needs:** trading, condition
checks, receipt records and restricted employer funds.

## T1-05 — Yesterday's rent

**Issuer:** Ledger. **Kind:** off-book theft; repeatable.

> The tenants have last month's receipts. The office has this month's
> version of last month. Bring the office copy. We might learn what a room
> costs after somebody has already paid for it.

**Work:** recover the particular rent ledger named in the order from a
Charter Family office, keeping the relevant pages intact. Deliver it to the
assigned Quiet Book receiver. A random ledger will not do.

**Draft terms:** 450 marks; no Exchange bond; three real hours. Failure
forfeits pay and damages Quiet. Exposure can damage Standing and produce
consequences from the office; taking extra property is outside the order.

**Personal assignment:** reserve a specific ledger and case for one player.
Another offer selects different records. An object already stolen is not
reissued as though it were still in the office.

**Plays with:** climbing, vaulting and pickup. **Needs:** guarded access,
document objectives, receivers and exposure consequences.

## T1-06 — Say what was weighed

**Issuer:** Maro Teska. **Kind:** filed verification and rumour; repeatable.

> They say the hold is short of grain. They weighed one empty wagon and
> stopped looking. Check the three loads I have listed. Then tell the
> buyers what the scales said.

**Work:** obtain weight receipts for three assigned caravan lots, verify
them against the cargo, and lodge the corrected report with the named market
buyers. The order asks for evidence, not a favourable story invented for Maro.

**Draft terms:** 300 marks; 45-mark bond; three real hours. A correct report
raises Standing. Default loses bond and Standing. Falsified evidence opens
a dispute and can create a capped 300-mark liability.

**Personal assignment:** a distinct report and lot allocation. Shared prices
can respond to verified news, but another player's report is not this player's
receipt and cannot pay them again for the same verification.

**Plays with:** travel and talking. **Needs:** weight records, conversations,
verification and market effects.
